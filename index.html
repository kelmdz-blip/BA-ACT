ABOUT THE ARTIST

Vincent van Gogh

Vincent van Gogh was a Dutch post-impressionist painter known for his expressive brushwork, vivid colors, and emotional style.

Although he received little recognition during his lifetime, his paintings later became some of the most celebrated artworks in the world.

His unique use of color and movement continues to influence artists and inspire people around the world.
